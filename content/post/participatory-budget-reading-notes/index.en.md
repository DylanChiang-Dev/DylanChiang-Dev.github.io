 Participatory budgeting process and key points at each stage
 In the proposal review stage, there are four main links. The first is the convening of the residents' meeting. This part is for citizens to brainstorm public issues and their solution strategies in order to formulate proposal ideas, which will be voted on by the participating citizens. 
 
 This is followed by the first step in participatory budgeting: the two stages of the proposal review workshop. Among them, the first stage is the "proposal discussion" stage, where bureaucratic representatives of the responsible agencies communicate with the proposers about the proposed alternatives or reasons for their infeasibility, and then experts and scholars provide correction suggestions and precautions. 
 
 Next, representatives from the citizen review panel will review the proposal based on three indicators: publicity, legality, and budget feasibility. After completion, the proposal plan will be publicly displayed online or in person so that you can understand the proposal and express your opinions. 
 
 Proposals in the proposal voting stage must obtain "high" or "moderate" feasibility evaluations on all three indicators of proposal review before they can be voted on by the i-Voting system. 
 
 As for the third stage, the Civil Affairs Bureau will notify the agency responsible for voting on the proposals and plan the required budget. ## Chapter 17 How can one be punished for being the leader?

 After repeated deliberation, avoid the occurrence of liability situations
 The original intention of citizen participation is to share the decision-making process originally monopolized by the government with the people. When the democratic parties fail to fulfill their civic responsibilities in every aspect of participatory budgeting and the content of the proposals is of poor quality, although they do share the decision-making power from the administrative agencies, they may only share the power of wasting public funds. In this way, the good intentions of citizen participation are lost. 
 
 In order to ensure the quality of citizen participation in decision-making and avoid the situation of being the master of the country but being held accountable, the proposer should be allowed to return to the community to dialogue with the people. It is recommended that more physical communication channels be added during the proposal review stage, such as citizen cafes, public hearings, briefings, symposiums, workshops, on-site surveys, etc., so that the proposer can promote the concept, persuade the public, and ensure that the proposal meets the needs of the community.

## Chapter 22 SOP for Participatory Budgeting
 Ke Wenzhe stated the two major features of Taipei City’s promotion of participatory budgeting:
 
 The focus is on citizen education and bringing the new concept of direct democracy into Taipei City. 
 Civil servants are actually involved. During the process, civil servants are directly involved through courses and briefing sessions, and opportunities for contact with citizen groups are also increased. We strive to integrate this entire system into the public service system. 
 Citizen Proposal and Review Procedures

 Citizen Proposal and Review Procedures - District Level
 
 Standard SOP
 1. Proposal review
 The Taipei City Government organizes residents' meetings, inviting proposers and citizens who care about the proposal to discuss public issues, put forward improvement plans for the problems, and formulate proposal concepts, which are then voted on by on-site participants to finalize the proposal. 
 
 2. Proposal Review Workshop
 Due to the limited public understanding of the administrative process, if government bureaus are not involved to help evaluate the practical feasibility of the proposal before citizens enter the voting process, the subsequent implementation stage may be bumpy and difficult (Su Caizu, 2017). The first stage of the proposal workshop allows agency representatives to communicate face-to-face with the proposer to understand the actual situation, so that the follow-up process of the proposal can be smoothly promoted, and experts and scholars are invited to provide correction directions for the proposal content. In the second stage of proposal review, the citizen review panel will review the proposal content based on three indicators: "publicity", "legality" and "budget feasibility". 
 
 The Taipei City Participatory Budget Citizen Review Panel is composed of 12 members. The qualifications are as follows:
 
 2 members of the government’s Citizen Participation Committee (acting as moderator and co-moderator). 
 
 1 reviewer (serves as deputy moderator and assists the moderator in conducting meetings). 
 
 2 citizens with advanced cards. 
 
 5 citizens with entry-level cards. 
 
 There were 2 proposers in the previous year (excluding the proposers from this administrative region). 
 
 Indicators and signals for "publicity", "legality" and "budgetary feasibility" Based on the content of the proposal, application of regulations and budget, indicators for the degree of impact of the implementation of the proposal on "publicity", "legality" and "budgetary feasibility" are designed. Innovative indicators of "red", "yellow" and "green" are used to facilitate participating citizens to understand and check. Those with "high" or "moderate" feasibility of the three indicators can enter the subsequent voting process. 
 
 3. Public exhibition of proposals and plans
Before the proposal voting, the public exhibition process of the proposal plan will be carried out, including "online exhibition" and "physical exhibition". The public can have an in-depth understanding of the content of the proposal before the voting, and also have the opportunity to express their opinions on the proposal. 
 
 4. Proposal voting
 Through the participatory budget i-Voting process, those registered in Taipei City, or studying, working, or living in Taipei City, and who are over 16 years old, can vote online or go to the district office to authenticate and vote online. If the number of votes exceeds the district threshold and is within the upper limit of the record after sorting, the case can be recorded. 
 
 5. Budget evaluation, budget review and parliamentary supervision stages
 After the proposal is approved by voting, it will be officially recorded. The Civil Affairs Bureau will notify each responsible agency to conduct budget evaluation and planning. If there are many agencies involved, one agency will be designated as the main responsible agency (Project Manager). Those who have a budget for the current year can implement it first. If the proposal implementation funds need to be paid in the next year's budget, or if a new budget is compiled for the next year, it will be handled according to the budget preparation process, and then the budget approval and supervision will be carried out by the parliament. 
 
 Flexibility under standardization
 1. Convene an agency consensus meeting
 The Civil Affairs Bureau holds an agency consensus meeting every year to explain "improving the principles of designation and adjustment of responsible agencies," "coordinating matters to improve the feasibility of proposals," "budget planning schedules," and "notes on the implementation of proposals," etc., in order to improve consensus on the designation of responsible agencies before each district meeting, so that participating agency colleagues can understand the follow-up procedures and agency roles, and provide specific and detailed information for review team members to evaluate carefully. 
 
 2. Hold a pre-meeting meeting
 In order to confirm the responsible agency for the proposal as early as possible and conduct consultation or communication in advance, each district office holds a pre-deliberation workshop meeting after the residents' meeting to invite relevant agencies of the proposal and designate the sponsoring agency for each case. 
 
 3. Direct execution mechanism
 "Direct implementation" was added in 2018. Some proposals are relatively simple and belong to a single responsible authority. If the existing budget of the current year is executable and the implementation can be completed in the current year, on the premise of the proposer's consent and reaching a consensus with the responsible authority, there will be no subsequent proposal review and voting procedures. The agency will handle it in the current year, reducing the complicated process and completing the proposer's idea as soon as possible. 
 
 4. Evaluation and processing mechanism
 After years of operation, proposals such as changes in traffic signal seconds, sidewalk settings, parking space planning or environmental beautification are public in nature. In order to avoid personal factors affecting the overall concept of regional planning and urban aesthetics, the proposal was discussed and approved by the public participation working group meeting. Projects including "traffic management", "walking space", "environmental painting or beautification", etc., were submitted to the responsible authority for overall evaluation and processing with the approval of the proposer. The evaluation was completed before August of the year and reported to the working group meeting.

 Limitations of Taipei City’s push for participatory budgeting
 Limitations of municipal issues
 
 Municipal-level proposals are made in the form of issues, and special projects are being carried out to deal with issues of new immigrants and groups with disabilities. Currently, citizen proposals are limited to the district level. How can bureaus design topics and develop them from top to bottom, or allow citizens to propose bottom-up proposals? It still needs to be followed up to derive appropriate city-level topics. 
 
 Lack of online discussion model
 
 At present, participatory budgeting in Taipei City is still conducted through on-site participation and discussion. With the trend towards younger and more convenient participation, whether it can be discussed online is worth looking forward to developing a model that transcends time and space. During the COVID-19 epidemic, various video and digital platforms have developed rapidly. In 2021 and 2022, attempts have been made to conduct deliberation workshops and residents’ meetings via online video. In the future, we may further think about how to make digital a normal mode of participation. 
 
 Similar proposal content
 
 Participants in each residents’ meeting every year are new, and citizens who have participated in the past cannot know the contents of all proposals. As a result, some proposals have similar content or have been completed. Currently, descriptions of regional characteristics and successful cases are reported by each district office before the residents’ meeting. Strengthening local connectivity and avoiding similar proposal contents are also possible directions for future efforts.
