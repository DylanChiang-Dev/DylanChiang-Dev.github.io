## Speech Overview

This speech will explore the in-depth integration of artificial intelligence and social innovation, and analyze how to use AI technology to promote social progress and innovative development.

## Main content

### 1. Application of AI technology in social innovation
- Smart medical care and health management
- Educational technology and personalized learning
- Environmental protection and sustainable development
- Social service optimization

### 2. Innovative thinking and practical methods
- The importance of design thinking in AI applications
- The need for cross-sector cooperation
- Product development based on user needs
- Social impact assessment

### 3. Practical experience of Xiaoju Technology
- Company innovation project case sharing
- Balance between technology research and development and social responsibility
- Challenges and opportunities in the entrepreneurial process
-Team building and talent cultivation

### 4. How to become a "Super Creative Saiyan"
- Methods to cultivate innovative thinking
- Combination of technical ability and humanistic quality
- Continuous learning and self-improvement
- The importance of social responsibility

## Lecturer introduction

**Dylan Chiang**, founder of Xiaoju Technology, focuses on the application research and practice of AI technology in the field of social innovation. With rich entrepreneurial experience and technical background, he is committed to promoting the positive interaction between technology and society.
