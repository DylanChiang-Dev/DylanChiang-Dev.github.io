## Speech Overview

This speech will deeply explore the uniqueness and governance model of mainland China's Internet ecosystem, and analyze the interaction between platforms, algorithms and personal information risks.

## Main content

### 1. The particularity of mainland China’s Internet ecology
- Concepts of cyber sovereignty and digital governance
- The historical development trajectory of mainland Internet ecology
- Comparison with international Internet governance models
- Regulatory framework and policy evolution

### 2. Platform governance mechanism
- Roles and responsibilities of large technology platforms
- Content moderation and speech control
- Platform monopoly and competition policy
- Data governance and open strategy

### 3. Algorithm recommendation system
- The central role of algorithms in the platform economy
- How the recommendation algorithm works
- Algorithmic transparency and explainability
- Algorithmic bias and fairness issues

### 4. Personal data risk and protection
- Current status of personal data collection and use
- Privacy protection and regulatory challenges
- Data security and cross-border transmission
- User rights protection mechanism

### 5. Future trends and challenges
- Combination of artificial intelligence and network governance
- International cooperation and conflict
- Innovation development and risk control
- Sustainable development of digital society

## Lecturer introduction

**Dylan Chiang**, a cross-strait Internet and AI innovator, focuses on digital policy research and Internet ecological observation. With rich cross-field experience, he is committed to promoting cross-strait exchanges and understanding in the fields of network governance and digital innovation.
