## Lecture Overview

This lecture will be delivered by Mr. Dylan Chiang, an Internet and AI innovator on both sides of the Taiwan Strait, and will provide an in-depth discussion of the latest trends in smart city construction and Internet economic development in mainland China.

## Main content

### Current status of smart city development
- Policy framework for smart city construction in mainland China
- Smart practice cases in major cities
-Technological innovation and application scenario analysis

### Development Trend of Internet Economy
- The impact of digital economy on traditional industries
- Popularity of e-commerce and mobile payment
- Development model of platform economy

### Cross-Strait Cooperation Opportunities
- Technical exchanges on smart city construction
- Prospects for cooperation in the field of Internet economy
-Talent training and industry docking

## Lecturer introduction

**Dylan Chiang**
Internet and AI innovators on both sides of the Taiwan Strait have long been concerned about the development of the digital economy in mainland China and have rich experience in smart city construction and Internet economic research.
