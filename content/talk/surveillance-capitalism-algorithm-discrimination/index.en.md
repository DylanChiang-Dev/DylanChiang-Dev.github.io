## Speech Overview

This speech will systematically analyze the phenomenon of surveillance capitalism and algorithmic discrimination in mainland China's Internet ecosystem from the intersection of political economy and technological sociology.

## Main content

### 1. Theoretical Framework of Surveillance Capitalism
- Concept and characteristics of surveillance capitalism
- Data as a new production factor
- Predict commodification and behavior modification
- Comparative analysis with traditional capitalism

### 2. The practice of surveillance capitalism in mainland China
- Data collection mechanism for large platforms
- Business model of personalized recommendation system
- Combination of government data and platform data
- Digital RMB and behavior tracking

### 3. Types and mechanisms of algorithmic discrimination
- Definition and classification of algorithmic discrimination
- Discriminatory results in recommendation algorithms
- Bias in credit assessment systems
- Recruitment and credit discrimination cases

### 4. The formation mechanism of discrimination
- Bias in training data
- Influence of values of algorithm designers
- The amplifying effect of systemic bias
-Feedback loop and prejudice solidification

### 5. Social Impact and Consequences
- Impact on personal rights and interests
- Rising social inequality
- Commercialization of public space
- Impact of democratic participation

### 6. Regulatory and governance challenges
- Inadequacies in the existing legal framework
- Difficulty of technical supervision
- The need for international coordination
- Balance between innovation and regulation

### 7. Reform paths and suggestions
- Transparent algorithmic governance
- Diversified technology development
- Public participation mechanism
- Improved rights protection system

## Lecturer introduction

**Dylan Chiang**, a cross-strait Internet and AI innovator, focuses on digital policy research and Internet ecological observation. Analyze platform economy and digital governance issues from the perspective of political economics, and strive to promote fairness and justice in the digital society.
