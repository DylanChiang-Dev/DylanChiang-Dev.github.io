## Meeting Overview

On the afternoon of July 8, the economic sub-forum of the 10th Cross-Strait Student Forum, hosted by the Collaborative Innovation Center for the Peaceful Development of Cross-Strait Relations and the Straits Exchange and Cultural Center and hosted by the Taiwan Institute of Xiamen University, was successfully held in the Keli Building of Xiamen University.

## Participating institutions

### Mainland universities
- Nankai University
- Wuhan University
- Xiamen University
- Jinan University
- Shenzhen University
-Lanzhou University
- Guangxi Normal University
- Guangxi University for Nationalities

### Universities in Taiwan
- Chinese Culture University
- Tamkang University
- I-Shou University

## Meeting theme

This sub-forum is divided into the first half and the second half, each focusing on the following two themes:

### First half theme
**Restructuring of the global value chain and cross-strait economic and trade relations**

### Second half theme
**Chinese-style modernization and cross-strait economic integration development**

## Host guest

- **Professor Wang Hua** - Institute of Economics, Taiwan Research Institute, Xiamen University (hosted in the first half)
- **Associate Professor Wang Yong** - Director of the Institute of Economics, Taiwan Research Institute, Xiamen University (hosting the second half)

## Meeting results

The young students briefly elaborated on the topic background, research perspective, data selection, model construction, research conclusions and other aspects of their published papers. Experts and scholars made professional comments on the report content of students from both sides of the Taiwan Strait, and the participating students benefited a lot.

This forum provides a valuable academic exchange platform for young scholars from both sides of the Taiwan Strait and promotes in-depth dialogue and cooperation between the two sides in the economic field.
