## Workshop Overview

On December 3, 2018, I participated in the academic seminar on Sun Yat-sen Thought and Contemporary National Development sponsored by the Institute of National Development and Mainland China at the Chinese Culture University to conduct an in-depth discussion on the guiding significance and practical value of Sun Yat-sen Thought in contemporary times.

## Organizer

**Institute of National Development and Mainland China, Chinese Culture University**

## Meeting location

**Father of the Sun Memorial Hall**

## Meeting theme

In-depth discussion of the guiding significance and practical value of Sun Yat-sen Thought in contemporary times

## Main topics

### 1. Theoretical connotation of Sun Yat-sen Thought
-The formation and development of the Three People's Principles
- Combination of revolutionary theory and practice
- Core elements of the ideological system

### 2. The contemporary value of Zhongshan Thought
- The guiding ideology of modernization construction
- The development path of democratic politics
- Theoretical support for national rejuvenation

### 3. Sun Yat-sen Thought and National Development
- Innovation in nation-building theory
- Regional development coordination mechanism
- The source of wisdom for reform and opening up

### 4. The practical inspiration of Zhongshan Thought
- Modernization of governance system
- Social fairness and justice
- Path of peaceful development

## Academic exchange

Conduct in-depth exchanges with scholars and experts from various universities and research institutions on the contemporary value of Sun Yat-sen Thought, and jointly explore its guiding significance in the construction of a modern country.

## Academic significance

This seminar will help deepen the understanding of Sun Yat-sen Thought and provide theoretical reference and practical inspiration for contemporary national construction and development.
