## Meeting Overview

**Meeting Name:** The 3rd Cross-Strait Forum on Rural Revitalization and Common Prosperity
**Conference Time:** June 12-15, 2025
**Meeting location:** Xiamen
**Sponsor:** Institute of Rural Development, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences
**Meeting theme:** Rural revitalization and common prosperity

## Meeting objectives

This forum aims to promote cross-Strait exchanges and cooperation in the field of rural revitalization, explore paths and mechanisms to achieve common prosperity, and contribute wisdom and strength to promote the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.

- **Full day:** Visiting and inspecting villages around Xiamen
