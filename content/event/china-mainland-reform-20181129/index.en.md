## Workshop Overview

On November 29, 2018, I participated in the 40th Anniversary Academic Seminar on "Mainland China's Reform" hosted by the Institute of National Development and Mainland China, Academy of Social Sciences, Chinese Culture University.

## Organizer

**Institute of National Development and Mainland China, School of Social Sciences, Chinese Culture University**

## Meeting theme

Conduct in-depth academic discussions and exchanges around the 40 years of reform and opening up in mainland China.

## Main topics

### 1. The historical process of reform and opening up
- The beginning and breakthrough of reform
- Open scope and levels
- Key nodes and major decisions

### 2. Development achievements and experience
- Economic growth miracle
- Comprehensive social progress
- Improvement of overall national strength
- Institutional innovation achievements

### 3. Theoretical contribution and significance
- Theory of socialism with Chinese characteristics
- The formation of the theory of reform and opening up
- Inspiration for world development

### 4. Challenges and opportunities in the new era
- New topics for deepening reform
- High-quality development requirements
- New journey of modernization construction

## Participating Scholars

Experts and scholars from universities and research institutions on both sides of the Taiwan Strait conducted in-depth exchanges and discussions on relevant issues.

## Academic value

This seminar provides an important academic exchange platform for scholars from both sides of the Taiwan Strait and promotes systematic understanding and research on the development process of the 40 years of reform and opening up.
