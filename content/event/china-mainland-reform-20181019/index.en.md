## Workshop Overview

On October 19, 2018, participated in an international seminar on the theme of reform and peaceful development in mainland China.

## Meeting theme

Focusing on the summary of the experience of reform and opening up in mainland China, the future development path, and the discussion of the peaceful development model.

## Participating institutions

Experts and scholars from academia and policy research institutions participated in the discussion.

## Main topics

- Achievements and experience in 40 years of reform and opening up
- Mainland China’s development path in the new era
- Peaceful development and international cooperation
- Regional development and cooperation opportunities

## Meeting results

Through this seminar, we can gain an in-depth understanding of the theory and practice of reform and development in mainland China, and provide a valuable platform for relevant policy research and academic discussions.
