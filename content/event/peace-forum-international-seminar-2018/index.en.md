## Workshop Overview

On October 7, 2018, I participated in the Peace Forum International Seminar hosted by the School of Social Sciences of Chinese Culture University, where I gathered with scholars and experts from home and abroad to discuss major issues of peaceful development.

## Organizer

**School of Social Sciences, Chinese Culture University**

## Meeting theme

Peace Forum International Seminar - Discussing peaceful development, regional cooperation and international relations

## Main topics

### 1. Concept of peaceful development
- Theoretical basis for peaceful development
- The value of peace in international relations
- The importance of regional peace and stability
- Civilized dialogue and exchanges and mutual learning

### 2. Regional cooperation mechanism
- Regional economic integration
- Security Cooperation Framework
- Cultural exchange and cooperation
- Common development path

### 3. New trends in international relations
- Adjustments and changes in international order
- Evolution of major power relations
- Multilateralism and cooperation
- Globalization and regionalization

### 4. Peacebuilding Practice
- Conflict prevention and resolution
- Building peace paths
- Humanitarian assistance
- International coordination mechanism

### 5. Future Outlook
- Opportunities and challenges for peaceful development
- Deepening of regional cooperation
- International governance innovation
- Building a community with a shared future for mankind

## Participating Scholars

Experts and scholars from domestic and foreign academic institutions and research organizations conducted in-depth exchanges on issues related to peaceful development.

## Academic significance

This seminar provides an important platform for peaceful development research, promotes in-depth discussions on peace issues in the academic community, and contributes wisdom to the promotion of peaceful development.
