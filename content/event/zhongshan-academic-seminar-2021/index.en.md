## Workshop Overview

Participate in the Zhongshan Academic Seminar hosted by the School of Social Sciences of National Chengchi University and conduct academic exchanges with scholars and experts from the Schools of Social Sciences of various schools.

## Organizer

**School of Social Sciences, National Chengchi University**

## Participating institutions

- School of Social Sciences, National Chengchi University
- School of Social Sciences, Chinese Culture University

## Meeting location

**National Chengchi University Comprehensive Hall International Conference Hall**

## Main topics

### 1. Theoretical research on Zhongshan Thought
- The core connotation of Zhongshan Thought
- The formation process of academic theory
- Structural analysis of ideological systems

### 2. The historical value of Sun Yat-sen Thought
- Position in historical development
- Impact on academia
- Implications for later research

### 3. The contemporary significance of Sun Yat-sen Thought
- Guiding values in modern society
- The significance of linking theory with practice
- Inspiration for future development

### 4. Academic research methods
- Perspectives on the study of Zhongshan Thought
- Interdisciplinary research methods
- Data analysis and interpretation

## Academic exchange

The participating scholars and experts conducted in-depth discussions on various aspects of Sun Yat-sen Thought, shared research experiences and academic achievements, and jointly promoted the development of research on Sun Yat-sen Thought.

## Academic value

This seminar provides an important communication platform for scholars engaged in the study of Sun Yat-sen thought, helping to deepen relevant theoretical research and promote academic development.
