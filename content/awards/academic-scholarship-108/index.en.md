## Award Overview

In the first semester of the **108 academic year**, I won the **Institute Academic Excellence Scholarship** (Certificate No.: 1090001009). This scholarship is designed to reward graduate students who have outstanding academic research and academic performance.

## Application conditions

Graduate Academic Merit Scholarships are generally awarded to students who meet the following criteria:
- **Excellent academic performance**: Academic performance ranks among the best in the department
- **Excellent Research Ability**: Demonstrate outstanding potential in the research field
- **Actively participate in academic activities**: Actively participate in seminars, lectures, etc.
- **Good Conduct**: Have good academic ethics and character

## Meaning of award

Receiving this scholarship is not only an affirmation of personal academic efforts, but also an incentive to continue to improve in the future research path. At the same time, it also demonstrated the potential and achievements in the field of forward-looking research.

## Academic achievements

During the award period, he actively invested in academic research and made important progress in related fields, laying a solid foundation for subsequent academic development.

## Scholarship Value

This scholarship not only provides financial support, but more importantly, it recognizes academic achievements and provides important encouragement and motivation to continue in-depth research.
