## Award Overview

The WeiShi project received a Bronze Award at the Sixth Yunnan-Taiwan University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition. I served as team lead and was primarily responsible for product and technology, turning the proposition “AI avatars connect first, while people retain the final decision” into an offline-demonstrable AI relationship-exploration prototype.

## Project and Role

- **Project:** WeiShi (未識)
- **Role:** Team lead
- **Primary responsibilities:** Product definition, system design, prototype implementation, governance mechanisms, and result integration

## Competition Context

The Yunnan-Taiwan University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition promotes exchange and collaboration among students across the Taiwan Strait. WeiShi uses AI avatars to support mutual understanding before an in-person interaction while preserving authorization, observability, and the person's final decision.
