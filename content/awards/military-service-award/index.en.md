## Award Overview

Being selected as the outstanding male service member in the first half of **112 (document number: 1121070500), this is a formal recognition of civil servants who have performed outstandingly during their alternative service.

## Substitute background

The alternative service system is one of Taiwan's important military service systems. It aims to enable servicemen to develop their expertise in various fields of society, serve the country and society, and cultivate young people's sense of responsibility and mission.

## Selection criteria

Substitute merit-based draftees are usually awarded to draftees who meet the following criteria:
- **Positive service attitude**: Serious and responsible, proactive
- **Excellent Work Performance**: Excellent performance in various tasks
- **Good teamwork**: get along well with colleagues, collaborate and help each other
- **Strict Discipline**: Strictly abide by military disciplines and regulations

## Meaning of award

- **Official Certification**: Official commendation from the Ministry of the Interior, with high credibility
- **Moral Affirmation**: Reflects excellent character and service spirit
- **Practical Ability**: Demonstrated good organizational and execution capabilities
- **Social Contribution**: Made a positive contribution to the country's society

## Service experience

During his service in the alternative service, he actively participated in various tasks and won unanimous praise from his unit supervisor and colleagues with his professional attitude and professionalism.
