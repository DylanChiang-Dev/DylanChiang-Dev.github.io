## Award Overview

Received a Bronze Award at the Fifth Yunnan-Taiwan University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition (第五屆雲台大學生雙創賽), jointly organized by Yunnan University and Ming Chuan University under the guidance of the Taiwan Affairs Office of the Yunnan Provincial People's Government and the Yunnan Provincial Department of Education.

## Competition background

The Yunnan-Taiwan University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition promotes exchange and cooperation among students across the Taiwan Strait, develops internationally minded innovators, and supports sustained youth exchange.

## Meaning of the award

- **Bronze Award Level**: Stand out in the fierce competition and demonstrate excellent innovation ability
- **Cross-Strait Cooperation**: Promote academic exchanges and cooperation between young people in Yuntai and Taiwan
- **Innovative Spirit**: embodies the spirit of continuous innovation and practice

## Entries

This competition brings together outstanding students from many universities on both sides of the Taiwan Strait. The participating projects cover a variety of fields, demonstrating the innovative vitality and entrepreneurial potential of the younger generation.
