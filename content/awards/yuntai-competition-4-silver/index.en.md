## Award Overview

Received a Silver Award at the Fourth Yunnan-Taiwan University Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition (第四屆雲台大學生雙創賽). The project was recognized by the judging panel for its innovation and market potential.

## Contest Features

- **Held cross-strait**: Co-organized by Yunnan University and Mingchuan University
- **Official Guidance**: Guidance from the Taiwan Affairs Office of the Yunnan Provincial People's Government and the Yunnan Provincial Department of Education
- **Academic Exchange**: Promote the collision of innovative thinking among college students from both sides of the Taiwan Strait

## Meaning of award

- **Higher Level**: The Silver Award is second only to the Gold Award, reflecting the top level
- **Competency Certification**: authoritative recognition by a professional jury
- **Cooperation Opportunities**: Lay the foundation for future cooperation

## Project Features

This award-winning project combines innovative thinking and practical application, demonstrates the creativity and execution ability of Yuntai young students, and provides an important platform for promoting in-depth exchanges between young people from both sides of the Taiwan Strait.
