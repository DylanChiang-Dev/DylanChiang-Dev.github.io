## About me

I am a doctoral student in intelligent science and technology at the School of Big Data at Fudan University. I joined the Data Intelligence and Social Computing Laboratory (Fudan DISC) and serve as the chairman of the Chinese AI Application Development Association (CAIADA). Research focuses on natural language processing, large-scale language models and computational social science, and how artificial intelligence serves research, knowledge work and social governance.

I have a research background in national development, public administration and artificial intelligence adoption. From the use of generative AI in political work to trustworthy AI tools for researchers in the humanities and social sciences, I continue to explore how to combine computational methods, awareness of social science issues, and human judgment.
